What we do

Residential and light commercial work, all done to code with permits handled for you.

  • Panel upgrades

    Replace old fuse boxes and undersized panels so your home can handle what you plug in.

  • Outlets and circuits

    New outlets, dedicated circuits for appliances, and GFCI protection for kitchens and baths.

  • Lighting

    Recessed lights, fixtures, ceiling fans, and outdoor and landscape lighting.

  • EV chargers

    Level 2 charger installs, including any panel or circuit work the charger needs.

  • Generators and surge protection

    Standby generator hookups and whole-home surge protectors.

  • Troubleshooting and repairs

    Breakers that keep tripping, dead outlets, flickering lights. We find the cause and fix it.
